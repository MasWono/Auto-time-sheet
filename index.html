Rate Settings

Rates and records are stored only on this device. No name, employee ID, company account, or login is required.

31-Day Rule

If a 31-day month has no H, the daily-rate portion is capped at 30 days. OT recorded on day 31 is carried forward. If the month contains H, the month is calculated normally.

February Rule

February is always paid as 30 daily-rate days, even when the actual number of working days is fewer than 30.

GRI Rule

GRI earned from P days is paid in the following month's Time Sheet. Example: September P days → October Previous GRI.
